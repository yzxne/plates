Plates privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Plates is a personal nutrition and workout tracker built for one person's own use. It is not offered to the public.

What Plates stores

  • Food, weight, workout and settings data you enter.
  • This data is saved in a Google Sheet that Plates creates in your own Google Drive.

Google access

  • Plates asks only for the drive.file permission: it can see and edit only the files it creates. It cannot see your other Drive files, email or contacts.
  • The access token is stored encrypted on the Plates backend (Cloudflare Workers) and is never shared or sold.
  • You can remove access at any time in your Google Account → Security → Third-party connections, or with "Disconnect Google" in the app.

Other services

  • Food searches go to USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts. Meal photos, if you use AI meal scan, are sent to Anthropic for analysis. Only the data needed for that request is sent.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no data sales.

Deleting your data

Delete the Plates spreadsheet from your Google Drive and disconnect Google. Nothing else is kept.
